# Privacy

BBSFusion is designed as a local-only Android app.

- There is no BBSFusion cloud service.
- The app does not upload credentials, cookies, forum content, private messages, browsing history, analytics, telemetry, or crash reports.
- Login is handled through the original forum pages in Android WebView.
- Forum cookies are stored by Android WebView/CookieManager on the device.
- Network requests are initiated by explicit user actions such as opening a group, refreshing a list, opening a topic, or opening the original site.

The app connects directly to the selected forums and image hosts needed to display forum content. Those services remain governed by their own privacy policies and terms.
